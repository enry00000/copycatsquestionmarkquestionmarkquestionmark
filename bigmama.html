Flying Lotus - BIG MAMA / Review

6.3/10

electronic / idm / glitch - brainfeeder - march 6, 2026
by enrico elio scollante

Flying Lotus goes back in time and decides to release an EP of pure experimentation in the world of IDM and glitch music.
The final product, however, fails to fully achieve its goal of being relevant in a genre that today thrives in its underground niches,
from which, in my opinion, it shouldn't be "allowed" to emerge much (said by someone who is part of those niches).
