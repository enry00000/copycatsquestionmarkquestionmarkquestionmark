About copycats.

"copycats" is an online label and magazine, opened in november 2025 by enrico elio scollante.


conceptually inspired by the new era of online music networking and by both the
visual and musical comeback of the electronic scenes of the 90s, "copycats" was
born from the influence of labels such as Warp Records and Rephlex Records,
and takes most of its visual identity from design studios like The Designers Republic and Blue Source.


"after passing a few years going around music communities and knowing
different artists, i felt like i needed to create myself a space to share the great music
that lies in online communities and to talk about the stuff that inspires me and write
about new music that comes out and don't really get that much of attention outside
of the internet."
